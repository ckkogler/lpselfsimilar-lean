# Mathematical sources

- Samuel Kittle and Constantin Kogler, *On Lp density of self similar measures
  and Bernoulli convolutions*. Primary source for the higher-integrability
  theorem, exponent-100 criterion and revised quantitative supporting lemmas.
  Its introduction also states the cubic criterion and rational consequence.
- Samuel Kittle and Constantin Kogler, *A cubic criterion for continuous
  algebraic Bernoulli convolutions*. Primary source for the exponent-three
  theorem, rational corollary, working regularity criterion and its supporting
  arguments, including both appendices.
- Emmanuel Breuillard and Péter P. Varjú, *Entropy of Bernoulli convolutions
  and uniform exponential growth for linear groups*. The required Gaussian
  comparison and quantitative entropy consequences are proved internally.
  [Versioned preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.04043v4).
- Dongning Guo, Shlomo Shamai (Shitz) and Sergio Verdú, *Mutual information and
  minimum mean-square error in Gaussian channels*, IEEE Transactions on
  Information Theory **51** (2005), 1261–1282. The scalar binary Gaussian
  identity required here is proved directly for actual conditional
  expectations and variances; the general channel theorem is not assumed.

The primary works are identified by title and authors without asserting an
unverified public publication identifier. The first source is the revised
manuscript with the `18^d` reverse Hölder constant and the non-strict
exponent-100 criterion. These citations identify mathematical
sources; they are not proof axioms or runtime dependencies. All required source
results and inherited hypotheses are mapped in [COVERAGE.md](../COVERAGE.md).
